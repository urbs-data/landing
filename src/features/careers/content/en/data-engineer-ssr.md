At Urbs Data we support companies with data, BI, and process automation projects. We are looking for a Semi Senior Data Engineer to design and maintain data solutions on GCP, and who is comfortable talking with clients to understand what they need.

## What you will do

- Develop and maintain ETL pipelines and data models.
- Model and transform data with dbt on BigQuery.
- Build and maintain dashboards and reports in Metabase.
- Take part in client conversations to gather requirements and validate solutions.
- Document solutions, decisions, and processes.

## What we are looking for

- Experience as a Data Engineer (semi senior level).
- Experience in data warehousing and ETL development.
- Knowledge of Data Lakes and their architecture.
- Advanced SQL.
- Experience with dbt.
- Experience with BigQuery and GCP.
- Experience with Metabase.
- Experience with Airbyte and Airflow.
- Experience with Google Workflows and Cloud Run.
- Knowledge of CDC (Change Data Capture).
- Experience integrating data from APIs and databases.
- Experience with CI/CD and deployment automation.
- Comfort talking with clients and explaining technical topics to business stakeholders.

### Language

- Conversational English (B2).
