At Urbs Data we support companies with data, BI, and process automation projects. We are looking for a Data Lead who makes strategic decisions on projects, leads the team, and acts as the bridge between business and technology.

## What you will do

- Lead data projects end to end, defining architecture, priorities, and strategy.
- Coordinate and grow a team of 2 to 3 people or more.
- Take on the PM/PO role: build roadmaps, manage the backlog, and align expectations with clients.
- Document solutions, decisions, and processes.
- Take part in hiring and interviewing new data profiles.

## What we are looking for

### Leadership and management

- Experience leading data projects and making strategic decisions.
- Experience leading teams of more than 3 people.
- Experience as a PM or PO, including documentation and roadmap building.
- Command of agile methodologies.
- Experience interviewing or selecting data profiles.

### Technical

- ETL development.
- Knowledge of Data Lakes and Data Warehouses.
- Working with git.
- Nice to have: experience with GCP (BigQuery, Cloud Storage, Dataflow, etc.).
- Nice to have: experience with Metabase, Airflow, dbt, dlt.

### Language

- Conversational English (B2).
